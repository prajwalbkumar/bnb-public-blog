### How to Stay Focused

1. **Anchor to Your Audience’s Needs:** Always ask, _"Will this help AEC professionals adopt tech or improve their workflows?"_
2. **Define Your Content Pillars:**
    - AEC Automation (tools, coding, plugins).
    - Computational Design and Modeling.
    - Practical applications of tech in AEC (e.g., AI or SaaS).
3. **Iterate Based on Feedback:** Test which topics resonate most with your audience. Double down on what gains traction.
